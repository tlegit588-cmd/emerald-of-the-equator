BEFORE YOU GO

A few useful travel notes.

Use these as planning prompts, then confirm current details with official sources and your local host before departure.

01

Documents & entry

Check passport validity, visa or entry requirements, and any transit rules with the relevant official authorities for your nationality.

02

Getting around

Plan island transfers and road journeys ahead. Ask about boat schedules, road conditions, journey times and a trusted local driver or guide.

03

Health & preparation

Seek travel-health advice before departure, pack any prescribed medicines, insect protection and suitable footwear, and check local health guidance.

04

Travel with respect

Ask before photographing people, support local businesses, follow conservation guidance and leave beaches, forests and heritage sites as you found them.

This guide is general information, not a live directory or official travel advisory. Accommodation, restaurants, tours, transport and entry rules can change. Confirm availability, prices, safety and requirements directly before booking.
