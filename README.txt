EMERALD OF THE EQUATOR — SPRINT 4
Visitor Guide: Accommodation discovery, local food, experiences and practical travel notes.

FILES
- index.html: adds the Travel Guide navigation and new visitor-guide sections.
- styles.css: responsive layouts for stay, food, activities and travel notes.
- app.js: retained from Sprint 3; destination atlas, map, search, favourites, audio, coordinates and WhatsApp continue to work.
- i18n.js: retains Sprint 3 translations and adds Sprint 4 translations in English, Portuguese, French and Spanish.

DEPLOYMENT (GitHub + Cloudflare)
1. Download and extract this ZIP.
2. Open the existing emerald-of-the-equator GitHub repository.
3. Replace the repository-root files with ALL five files from this package: index.html, styles.css, app.js, i18n.js and README.txt. Do not upload the ZIP itself as the website.
4. Commit the changes to the same branch connected to the Cloudflare Worker.
5. Wait for the Cloudflare deployment to finish, then visit https://tourism.fehborganization.org/.
6. Test the Travel guide navigation, each new section, the EN/PT/FR/ES selector, the destination map and WhatsApp contact.

IMPORTANT EDITORIAL NOTES
- No accommodation, restaurant or tour operator is presented as a verified business listing. The guide offers discovery categories and invites visitors to request locally confirmed options from FEHB.
- Travel entry, health, transport and safety requirements change. The page deliberately tells visitors to confirm current requirements with official authorities and local providers. It is not an official travel advisory.
- Food examples are broad island-food discovery prompts, not a guaranteed menu at any particular venue.
- WhatsApp contact remains +239 986 0074 (https://wa.me/2399860074). FEHB parent website and logo links are retained.
- This is a static frontend. It does not process bookings, payments or availability checks.
